What happened in August

A quiet month. No new spending posted after August 7, so every expense on the books this year was already covered in the last report. The three checks to River Glen School for the art, music and science programs — $45,180 — remain the largest expense of the year, and those programs are fully paid for.

On the income side, a second $260 workplace-giving donation arrived August 26, a $117 merchandise deposit was made in branch August 24, and the savings account earned $513.78 in interest on August 31. Total interest for the year now stands at $1,043.16. Nothing posted to any account between September 1 and 8.

The $171.14 online store deposit flagged last month as missing from QuickBooks has since been recorded — it posted August 7 and was split between the donation drive and merchandise sales. That item is resolved. A corporate matching drive starts this fall, with Gala Night in March and Caminata in April.

For the board's attention

  • The savings account's 3.30% interest rate expires today, September 8. Watch the September statement for the new rate — at our current balance the difference is worth about $500 a month.
  • Check #5611 for $368.72, written June 25, is the last of the old checks still uncashed after nearly three months — worth checking in with the payee or considering reissuing.
  • A $55.52 check cleared as #5571 but was listed last month as #5573 — worth confirming with the bookkeeper which number is correct.
  • The donation drive budget shows $15,000 this year, down from the $18,000 used in last month's report — worth confirming this was an intentional change.
