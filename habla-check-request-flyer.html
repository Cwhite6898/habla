How to Get Reimbursed

Four steps to request a check from HABLA

Spent your own money on something for River Glen, or need HABLA to pay a vendor? Here’s how to get the check. Start with the Check Request form — it’s a fillable PDF, linked in step 1 below.

1

Fill out the Check Request form

Your name and contact info, the event and date, what you bought, the amount, and who the check should be made out to.
Download the 2026–27 Check Request form (PDF)

2

Attach your receipts or invoice

The attachments have to add up to the exact amount you’re asking for. If you paid out of pocket, attach your receipts. If HABLA is paying a vendor directly, attach their invoice.

3

Get your chair’s signature

Whoever runs the program or event you spent the money on signs the approval section — that confirms it’s in the budget.

4

Turn it in to the Treasurer

Email the completed form and your receipts to treasurer@hablariverglen.org. Send the form and each receipt as separate attachments in one email.

2 weeks Plan on up to two weeks from the day you turn it in. Need it sooner, or by a specific event date? Say so on the form.

What slows a request down

  • No receipt, or a receipt that doesn’t match the amount
  • Missing the approval signature

Good to know

  • Choose pickup or mail on the form. Pickup checks are held by Bertha in the school office; mailed checks need an address.
  • Please cash your check within 3 months.
