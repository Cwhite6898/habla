What happened in July

Three checks to River Glen School for the art, music and science programs came to $45,180 — that is 97% of everything spent so far this year, and those programs are now fully paid for. The rest was annual renewals for insurance, the website and Zoom, a state filing fee, and a $300 board-approved GoFundMe contribution for a school family.

On the income side, a $260 donation arrived through workplace giving and the savings account earned $529.38 in interest. Fundraising season starts with Caminata in the fall.

For the board's attention

  • The savings account's 3.30% interest rate ends September 8. At our current balance that is worth about $500 a month.
